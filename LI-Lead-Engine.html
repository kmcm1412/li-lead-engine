What this is

Every new business in New York must file with the NY Department of State. Those filings are public record, published as free open data and updated daily. This tool pulls every brand-new LLC and corporation registered in Suffolk and Nassau County — often within a day or two of filing.

Why it matters: a brand-new business needs insurance right now — general liability, a BOP, workers' comp, commercial auto — and most haven't picked an agent yet. You'll usually be the first agent to reach them, because you're seeing filings before they show up anywhere else.

The 20-minute daily routine

  1. Refresh Leads each morning (Suffolk, last 7–14 days).
  2. Scan the high-priority ones — contractors, trucking, restaurants, day cares, salons, landscapers. These have mandatory coverage needs (NY requires workers' comp & disability the moment they hire anyone).
  3. Click 🔍 Google to find their phone/website, then + Pipeline to track them.
  4. Send the congratulations letter (Scripts tab) to 5–10 per day — the filing address is a valid mailing address. Or call the ones you can find numbers for.
  5. Work the Pipeline tab: it sorts by follow-up date so nothing slips.

Getting the most out of each feature

  • 📅 Today tab — open the app, and it tells you what to do: follow-ups due, brand-new hot leads, and your month against goal. Set your monthly premium goal and commission rate in ✉️ Scripts & Letters.
  • X-dates win commercial accounts. Most businesses you call already have coverage — that's fine. Ask one question: "When does it renew?" Log it in the account's X-date field, click ⏰ 60 days before, and the account resurfaces on your Today tab exactly when it's winnable. A book of 100 logged x-dates is a permanent prospecting machine.
  • 🖨️ Letters & 🏷️ labels — tick the ✉ box on the leads you want (or filter first and select none to use all shown), then print ready-to-sign letters and an Avery 5160 label sheet. Ten letters a day takes about 15 minutes including stuffing envelopes.
  • 🔍 Registry lookup — someone calls in or gets referred? Search their business name to see their entity, filing date, and address before you pick up the quote.
  • Cross-sell chips — every commercial account owner also has a home, cars, and a family. Tick Home/Auto/Life/Umbrella as you place them; an account with 3+ lines almost never leaves.

Why these leads are better than purchased leads

  • Free. Internet lead vendors charge $20–$60+ per shared lead that 5 other agents also bought. These cost nothing and nobody else is systematically working them.
  • Timely. You see the filing within days. The business owner is actively setting things up — bank account, EIN, insurance.
  • Commercial lines. New-business policies (GL/BOP/WC/commercial auto) carry higher premiums than personal auto, renew annually, and open the door to the owner's home, auto, and life business later.

Compliance quick notes

  • This is all public government data — no scraping, no privacy issues.
  • Mail is always fine. The congratulations letter is the safest and highest-converting first touch.
  • Calling a business about business insurance is a business-to-business call; the federal Do-Not-Call registry covers residential/personal numbers, not B2B calls. Many of these addresses are home-based businesses — if a number appears personal, lead with mail instead. Follow Allstate's own telemarketing guidelines.
  • Don't text prospects without prior consent.

Notes on the data

  • Source: NY Dept. of State "Active Corporations" dataset on data.ny.gov (official state open-data portal).
  • The address shown is the "process address" — where legal papers go. It's usually the owner's home or the business location; either way it's a valid mailing address.
  • Some filings list a registered-agent company (LegalZoom etc.) instead of the owner's address — those are marked and less useful for mail, but still worth a Google.
  • An internet connection is required to refresh leads. Your pipeline and notes are saved on this device — and if you set a sync code (Scripts & Letters tab), they also sync privately across your phone and computer.
  • 📬 A fresh daily lead digest is posted every weekday morning — and can be emailed to you automatically.
