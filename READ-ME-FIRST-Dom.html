🛡️ LeadEngine LI — Built for You, Dom

Free, automatic personal & commercial insurance leads — Long Island, Queens & NY State.
The idea in one sentence: home purchases and new-business registrations are public record — this tool watches both feeds and puts every new homeowner in Queens and every new business in Suffolk & Nassau on your screen, scored, qualified against your writing criteria, and ready to mail or call.
Built around your auto writing box: NY only · max 1 at-fault accident in 3 years · no DWI in 5 years · 12 months continuous coverage, no lapse · homeowners not on or near the water · renters, boats & motorcycles = easy adds. The ✅ Qualify tab and your public quote form both check all of it automatically — the coastal towns (Oakdale, Sound Beach, the Rockaways…) are flagged for you too.

Getting started (30 seconds, nothing to install)

  1. On any device — phone, office computer, laptop — go to:
    kmcm1412.github.io/li-lead-engine
  2. Bookmark it (on your phone: Share → "Add to Home Screen" and it works like an app).
  3. Click Refresh Leads. That's it — you're looking at every business registered in Suffolk County in the last two weeks.

No account, no password, no subscription, no software. It pulls straight from the official NY Department of State public registry and it will keep working every day, forever, for free.

What it does

  • 📅 Today — your morning command center: follow-ups due, the newest individual homeowner leads, fresh business filings, and your month tracked against a premium goal with estimated commission.
  • 🏠 New homeowner leads (Queens) — every recorded home purchase: buyer name, address, sale price. Investor/LLC purchases filtered out, coastal areas flagged 🌊. New owners are the perfect home + auto bundle quote — especially before their first renewal.
  • ✅ Qualify — run any caller or referral through your exact criteria in 30 seconds; it renders a verdict, then saves them to your pipeline with the notes pre-written.
  • 📥 Your own quote form — a personal "Get My Quote" web page with your name on it. Text the link, post it in local Facebook groups, put it in your email signature. Every submission asks your qualifying questions (at-fault accidents, DWI, prior coverage) and lands on your Today screen with the person's name, phone, and email — pre-qualified inbound auto leads, free, forever.
  • 🔎 Find Leads — live feed of new LLCs & corps in Suffolk/Nassau. Automatically flags the good ones (contractors, trucking, restaurants, day cares…) and tells you which lines they likely need — WC, GL, BOP, commercial auto. Plus a registry search for looking up any business that calls in.
  • 🖨️ One-click mail campaigns — tick the leads you want, hit print: ready-to-sign congratulation letters on your letterhead info, and Avery 5160 label sheets. A 10-letter daily mailing takes ~15 minutes.
  • 📋 My Pipeline — track status (New → Contacted → Quoting → Won), notes, follow-up dates, premium, current carrier, and x-date. One click sets a reminder 60 days before any prospect's renewal — the window where commercial accounts are actually won.
  • ✉️ Scripts & Letters — congratulations letter, cold-call opener, email template, and an x-date script — pre-filled with your name and the agency's number.

Why this beats buying leads

Internet lead vendors charge $20–$60 per lead — shared with five other agents.
This feed surfaces roughly 700+ new Suffolk businesses every two weeks, free and exclusive-by-neglect: almost no one works these systematically.

The math: mail 10 congratulations letters a day → ~200/month. Even at a modest 2–3% response, that's 4–6 warm commercial conversations a month you didn't have before — and every new BOP/WC account is a future home, auto, and life cross-sell.

The 20-minute daily routine

  1. Morning coffee → open the tool → Refresh Leads (Suffolk, last 7 days).
  2. Add the 🔥 high-priority ones to your Pipeline.
  3. Mail 5–10 congratulations letters (filing address is a valid mailing address) and call any you can find numbers for.
  4. Check the Pipeline tab for today's follow-ups.

Good to know

  • 🏠 The homeowner feed arrives in batches. The city records deeds in bulk (roughly monthly), so some mornings it's quiet and then a few hundred new homeowners drop at once. That's how the source works — not a glitch. The leads stay fresh for mailing: a welcome letter lands perfectly anytime in the first couple of months after closing, right before their first renewal.
  • The deed feed covers Queens. Suffolk and Nassau county clerks don't publish deeds as open data. For Suffolk/Nassau personal lines, the play is: run every caller and referral through ✅ Qualify, work the auto x-date script on everyone, and remember every business owner in the 🏢 feed also has a home and cars to bundle.
  • No public list has accident history or prior insurance — that's private information. The Qualify tab asks those questions for you in 30 seconds, so nothing unwritable slips into a quote.
  • 📬 Every weekday morning a fresh lead digest is posted automatically — only the businesses that are new since the day before. Bookmark it or check it with your coffee.
  • All data is official public record from data.ny.gov — nothing scraped, nothing shady. Mailing them is 100% fine; calling a business about business insurance is a normal B2B call.
  • Your pipeline is saved privately on whichever device you use — your phone and office computer each keep their own list (they don't sync). Most agents just pick one main device; use Export CSV if you ever need to move it.
  • Full details are in the 📖 How It Works tab inside the tool.
