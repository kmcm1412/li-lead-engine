🛡️ Your New Lead Engine, Dom

A free, automatic source of commercial insurance leads for Suffolk & Nassau — built just for you.
The idea in one sentence: every new business on Long Island must register with NY State — those filings are public, updated daily, and almost nobody works them. This tool puts every one of them on your screen, scored and ready to contact, usually within a day or two of filing — before they've picked an insurance agent.

Getting started (30 seconds, nothing to install)

  1. Save the file LI-Lead-Engine.html somewhere easy (your Desktop is fine).
  2. Double-click it. It opens in your web browser like a website.
  3. Click Refresh Leads. That's it — you're looking at every business registered in Suffolk County in the last two weeks.

No account, no password, no subscription, no software. It pulls straight from the official NY Department of State public registry and it will keep working every day, forever, for free.

What it does

  • 🔎 Find Leads — live feed of new LLCs & corps in Suffolk/Nassau. Automatically flags the good ones (contractors, trucking, restaurants, day cares…) and tells you which lines they likely need — WC, GL, BOP, commercial auto. One click to Google them or map the address.
  • 📋 My Pipeline — your working list. Track status (New → Contacted → Quoting → Won), notes, and follow-up dates so nothing slips. Everything saves automatically on your computer.
  • ✉️ Scripts & Letters — a mail-ready congratulations letter, cold-call opener, email template, and an x-date script — pre-filled with your name and the agency's number. Copy, paste, go.
  • 📤 Export CSV — one click gives you a mailing list you can hand to anyone for a letter mail-merge.

Why this beats buying leads

Internet lead vendors charge $20–$60 per lead — shared with five other agents.
This feed surfaces roughly 700+ new Suffolk businesses every two weeks, free and exclusive-by-neglect: almost no one works these systematically.

The math: mail 10 congratulations letters a day → ~200/month. Even at a modest 2–3% response, that's 4–6 warm commercial conversations a month you didn't have before — and every new BOP/WC account is a future home, auto, and life cross-sell.

The 20-minute daily routine

  1. Morning coffee → open the tool → Refresh Leads (Suffolk, last 7 days).
  2. Add the 🔥 high-priority ones to your Pipeline.
  3. Mail 5–10 congratulations letters (filing address is a valid mailing address) and call any you can find numbers for.
  4. Check the Pipeline tab for today's follow-ups.

Good to know

  • All data is official public record from data.ny.gov — nothing scraped, nothing shady. Mailing them is 100% fine; calling a business about business insurance is a normal B2B call.
  • Your pipeline lives only on your computer. If you switch computers, use Export CSV first.
  • Full details are in the 📖 How It Works tab inside the tool.
